How it works

  1. Review

    The agent checks messages and replies using your rules.

  2. Decide

    Open the email, handle the card, or dismiss it.

  3. Add context

    Notes and label corrections inform the next review.

Handling a card records a decision. It never sends, archives, deletes, or marks email read.
