How Email Desk works

Your priorities guide each review. Your feedback improves the next one.

  1. 01 / Review

    Read for context.

    The agent reads messages and replies against your rules, distinguishing open requests from receipts and routine updates.

  2. 02 / Surface

    See what matters.

    Relevant conversations become cards in Needs action or Worth knowing, with a reason and a link to the email.

  3. 03 / Refine

    Add your context.

    Handle or dismiss cards, correct labels, and add notes. Later reviews use your feedback and preserve your decisions.

A daily review rhythm. Morning and late-afternoon reviews run daily, plus midday on weekdays. The timeline shows planned times and confirmed updates.

Milan stays in control. Handling a card records your decision; it doesn’t send, archive, delete, or mark email read. Label corrections are checked at the next review.
