How Email Desk works

Email Desk pairs an AI agent with a simple attention board. You provide the context: what matters, how email should be categorized, and which conversations need follow-up. The agent uses that guidance each time it reviews your inbox.

  1. 01 / Review the conversations

    Read for context.

    The agent scans messages and replies against your priorities and organization rules. It categorizes the content and checks the conversation’s context, so an unanswered request is treated differently from a receipt or a newsletter.

  2. 02 / Publish the signal

    Two places for attention.

    The agent uses that context to decide what needs action and what is worth knowing. It publishes a short card for each relevant conversation, with a reason and a link back to the original email.

  3. 03 / Decide and refine

    Keep the next review informed.

    Handle or dismiss cards, correct labels, and add notes. The agent reviews that feedback on its next pass to refine what it surfaces, while preserving your decisions and completed work.

A daily review rhythm. The agent checks periodically throughout the day and brings new findings to the board at fixed morning, midday and late-afternoon checkpoints. You get a manageable view of incoming email at regular points in your day. The route shows the planned times and confirmed updates.

Milan stays in control. Handling a card records a decision. It does not send a reply, archive, delete, or mark email read. Feedback is reviewed before any authorized custom-label correction.
