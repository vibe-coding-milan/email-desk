Email Desk privacy notice
Updated November 21, 2026

Purpose and access

Email Desk is Milan Mijatovic's personal email attention board. The public demo
uses fictional conversations and does not connect to a visitor's mailbox.
The private reviewer is limited to the owner's two approved Google accounts;
there is no public mailbox-registration service.

Google data

The reviewer reads message headers, visible message text, thread context and
labels to classify mail and surface requests or useful updates. It reads the
owner's selected organization playbook in Google Docs. Google's authorization
scopes permit broader Gmail operations and Docs reading, but the hosted worker
provides only mail reads, changes to approved custom labels, and reading of the
fixed master playbook. It does not send, archive, delete or mark email read.

AI processing and hosting

Existing assistant-led reviews use the owner's connected tools. The
hosted reviewer sends bounded message excerpts, sender/subject information,
relevant thread context, organization rules, and matching board notes or
corrections to the OpenAI API to propose classifications and summaries. Requests
use store:false. Attachments are not fetched for this review; URLs and routine
authentication details are omitted from the model context where detected.

DreamHost hosts the authenticated board and the active private reviewer.
Stored card and feedback payloads are encrypted. Hosted Google grants and
review journals are encrypted and kept outside the public web directory.
Authorized local maintenance copies are kept in private project storage.
Technical usage records track review outcomes and model token use. Email Desk
does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train
general-purpose AI models.

Retention and control

Cards, owner notes, decisions and history persist across reviews. Handling or
dismissing a card does not delete the underlying email or the saved history.
Private maintenance records may be retained for verification and recovery.
The owner can stop reviewing, revoke the app's Google access in Google Account
settings, and request removal of stored Email Desk data. Revoking access stops
future access through that grant; it does not itself remove saved board data.
